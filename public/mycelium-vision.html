🏛️ The Empire Model

  • Central authority controls all access
  • Single password database = honeypot for attackers
  • Capture the king, control the kingdom
  • Admin privileges create "overlords"
  • Breach one system, compromise everyone
  • Trust flows top-down from authority

🍄 The Mycelium Model

  • Each node authenticates itself
  • No central database to steal
  • Remove any node, network persists
  • Every participant has equal sovereignty
  • Cryptographic proofs, not permissions
  • Trust emerges from verified connections

🔐 The Death of Passwords

Authentication is evolving from "prove you know a secret" to "prove you ARE who you claim."

❌ The Password Era (1960s-2020s)

Central databases of hashed secrets. Data breaches exposed billions. "Forgot password?" became a way of life. The emperor held the keys.

⚠️ The 2FA Transition (2010s-now)

Added a second factor, but still centralized. Now you need two things the emperor controls. Better, but still empire architecture.

✓ The Cryptographic Future (Now)

Wallet signatures. Self-sovereign identity. You don't prove you know a password — you prove you control your private key. No database to breach. No overlord required.

🌐 How The Dot Connector Implements Mycelium Auth
